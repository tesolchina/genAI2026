2. Deliverables at a glance

Argumentative research paper

  • Length = 500 words × number of members.
  • Format = plain text / Markdown / HTML (no PDF).
  • Structure = Title → Executive summary → Problem → Critical review (Issues 1 & 2; Issue 3 optional) → Complaints / Recommendations (2 required) → “How LegCo should follow up”.

Community engagement portfolio — 3 appendices (~100 words each + evidence)

  1. Writing for the public: SCMP (or other media) submission evidence + draft LegCo email. If not published, show your attempts and refinements.
  2. Communicating with government: all enquiries/responses, a timeline tracker, a short review of how officials handled the case.
  3. Engaging media/community: infographics or posts, screenshots + engagement metrics, a short reflection.

3. How AI can support your work (and how it cannot)

  • Use AI to draft sections, restructure arguments, check clarity against the template, and summarise evidence you have already collected.
  • Keep the trail: save your prompts and your edits — the portfolio declares your AI use.
  • Two human revision passes are required before submission; fact-check every claim, citation and figure yourselves.
  • Final voice and responsibility remain with your team. An AI-generated sentence you cannot defend should not be in the report.

4. Optional extra credit

  • After submitting, you may further develop the work: turn the report into a LegCo submission or reach out to the media (e.g. SCMP).
  • Not required for the core assessment — extra credit only.
