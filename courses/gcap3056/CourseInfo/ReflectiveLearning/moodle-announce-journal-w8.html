Announcement — Reflective Learning Journal (Weeks 8–10)

GCAP 3056 · Posted Week 8 (23 Oct 2026)

From this week we start the reflective learning journal (35% of your final grade, individual work). Three short windows — Weeks 8, 9 and 10 — one entry per week, about 150 words each.

The weekly routine (4 steps):

  1. Do the week's task below and reply in this forum thread.
  2. Draft your entry in your journal Google Doc — one tab per entry (Tab 3 = Week 8, Tab 4 = Week 9, Tab 5 = Week 10).
  3. Paste your doc link at the submission status page — status should read ok (if error, set the doc to “Anyone with the link can view”).
  4. When final, copy your entry into the Moodle forum thread to submit.

Week 8 — Entry 3 (required): Your learning setup and your partnership map

Answer all three parts (~150 words total):

  1. Website setup feedback. Look at the tools you actually used this week — gcap3056.simonsays.hk, the status page, your group's tracker page, Moodle, Google Docs. What works? What is confusing or slow? Name one concrete change that would help you get the job done.
  2. Partnership map. Pick one task you did this week. Write three short lines: I did… / AI did… / Peers and teacher did… Then say where you added something AI could not (judgment, context, a decision, a fix).
  3. Wow / Aha. One moment this week that surprised or impressed you (Wow), or one moment you saw how to apply a course idea to your project (Aha).

Week 9 — Entry 4 (optional, encouraged): From task to lasting change

Pick one: (a) before → after — how your approach to one type of task changed since the start of the course; (b) a failure worth keeping — what went wrong and what you will do differently; (c) your working method — the workflow you now rely on and why you trust it.

Week 10 — Entry 5 (optional, encouraged): Curate and commit

  1. Curate your strongest material from your Moodle replies and earlier tabs — the final set should read as your learning story.
  2. Commit: one paragraph on what habit or mindset you will still have next year.
  3. Submit all entries in the Week 10 thread by the deadline announced below.

Using AI — the rule

You are encouraged to use AI to draft, but you review, judge and revise every entry before submitting: no sentence you cannot explain, no claim you cannot check. How you divide the work between you, AI, peers and the teacher is itself one of the things we want you to reflect on.

Full guide + sample entry

See the course materials — Reflective Learning Journal (GitHub) — including a ~170-word sample entry you can imitate and a copy-paste AI drafting prompt.

Deadlines

  • Entry 3 (Week 8): required — this week.
  • Entries 4 & 5: optional but encouraged — by Week 10. Exact date and time to be confirmed on Moodle.
  • Early submissions welcome — submit any entry as soon as it is ready.
